## Adding an account could stop working for good

This release started with one bug report. Some people could not add any
account at all, whether by scanning a QR code or by typing the key. Looking for
the cause turned up more problems of the same kind, and this release fixes all
of them. None of them was a bug in how codes are calculated.

**What people saw.** Scanning a Google Authenticator export failed with
"Refusing to save 12 accounts over 22 already stored". Typing the key by hand
failed with "The account could not be saved. Please try again." Trying again
did not help.

**What had happened.** With sync on, the copy in your Google account can write
the same key differently from the copy on this computer: in lower case, or
with spaces. When the two copies were combined, the extension treated these
differently written keys as new accounts, and every account was saved twice.
The list still showed each one once, so nothing looked wrong.

The extension refuses any save that would leave fewer accounts than are already
stored. That rule exists so a bad read can never wipe your list. Here it
counted eleven accounts on screen plus the one being added, compared that with
the twenty-two stored, and refused every time.

**Your accounts were never at risk.** Every code kept working and nothing was
deleted. After this update, adding works again and you don't need to do
anything: the duplicates are merged the next time anything is saved. If a
Google Authenticator import failed for you, scan it again. Accounts you already
have are skipped, not added twice.

## Two windows at once

Since 1.13.0 the app can stay open as a side panel or a floating window, next
to the popup and the scanner tab. When two of them saved at nearly the same
moment, one save could overwrite the other. An account you had just added could
disappear, and one you had just deleted could come back. The cause was a
check that happened too early, with time left for another window to save in
between.

Now only one window can change your accounts at a time, and the others wait
their turn. The browser enforces this across every page of the extension,
including the background process.

The same fix closes a rarer and worse case: **turning off password
protection** at the moment another window saved an account could leave the
accounts encrypted with the key already removed. The password could not open
them, and neither could the recovery code. Turning protection on at the wrong
moment could also leave a copy of your keys unencrypted in sync. Neither can
happen now.

## Sync off now means off

On a computer where you had switched sync off, the extension still read
accounts from your Google account. If you then turned on password protection on
a different computer, the one with sync off picked up that password too, and
its own accounts ended up behind a password set somewhere else. Each save there
also deleted the copy stored in your Google account.

Now a computer with sync off leaves sync alone completely. As before, the moment
you switch sync off, your accounts are removed from Google's servers, as the
setting says.

## Moving in from other apps

- **New: import from Aegis, 2FAS, Bitwarden, FreeOTP+, andOTP and Raivo**
  export files, and from lists of `otpauth://` links. They go through the same
  **Import** button as our own backups.
- **An encrypted export from Aegis, 2FAS or Bitwarden** is refused with an
  explanation: export again without a password, then import that file.
- **Backups we can't read are refused instead of imported as junk.** Before
  this release, a file such as an encrypted Alibaba Cloud app backup was
  imported anyway. It produced rows named "Unknown" that showed no code. That
  backup is encrypted, and only the Alibaba Cloud app can open it. The
  extension now says so, and suggests adding each account by scanning its QR
  code.
- **Entries that can't make codes are never saved.** Counter-based (HOTP) and
  Steam entries used to be imported as ordinary 30-second codes, so the codes
  shown were wrong. They are now skipped, and the import says how many.
- **The import message tells you what happened.** It says how many accounts
  were added, how many were already in your list, and how many could not be
  read. Importing the same file twice no longer says "Import successful!".

## A row that says "Invalid key" can now be fixed

A row with a key that cannot make codes says "edit or remove". Until now the
Edit screen showed the key as read-only, so removing the row was the only
option. The key can now be corrected in place. It is checked the same way as
when you add an account.

## Codes that were wrong after the computer clock was fixed

If your computer's clock is off, the extension corrects for it. That correction
used to stay in use for up to two days, even after the clock itself had been
fixed. During that time codes were wrong, most visibly the codes quick fill
inserted. A correction now expires after five minutes unless a fresh check
confirms it. It is dropped as soon as the extension sees the clock jump.

## Also in this release

- **A deleted account no longer comes back.** When the app was open in a side
  panel or a floating window, deleting an account with sync on could restore it
  a moment later.
- **Setting up a new computer no longer cuts down the sync copy.** If you added
  an account there before your other accounts arrived from sync, the first sync
  could replace the copy in your Google account with that one account.
- **Damaged entries in storage no longer stop the app from opening.** An empty
  entry, or an entry with a missing or repeated id, used to break loading on
  every device that synced it. Dragging such a row could also delete another
  account. Damaged entries are now set aside and kept, never lost and never
  shown, and ids are repaired.
- **Error messages appear in your language.** Several screens, including the
  scanner, used to show the raw English text of an internal error.
- **Quick fill is in your language.** The "Insert 2FA code" right-click item
  and the notices quick fill shows on a page were in English for everyone,
  because the background part of the extension could not load translations.
- **Quick fill's keyboard shortcut fills the field you are in**, even when that
  field is inside an embedded frame. Before, it could fill the page around the
  frame, or copy the code to the clipboard.
- **Messages match what happened.** "Account added" appears only when an account
  was added. Saving an edit to an account that another window has already
  deleted says so. A search with no results shows "No accounts found". If
  turning sync back on fails, you are told. Failed edits, deletes and reorders
  are reported, and the edit screen stays open with what you typed.
- **The side panel picks up settings changed in the popup**, such as
  language, theme and switches, without having to be reopened.
- **A few more QR codes are accepted:** migration links written in capital
  letters, and account names that contain a `%` sign.
- **Exports always include algorithm, digits and period.** For some accounts
  these fields were missing, so the CXF export failed and the `.txt` export
  wrote `undefined`.

No permissions changed in this release. The store rollout follows separately, so
this version may be here before it reaches your browser.
